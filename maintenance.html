🗺️ Visual Maintenance Guide

The system on five diagrams. Full written detail lives in MAINTENANCE.md.

1 · How the whole system fits together

Two independent halves: static files served by GitHub Pages, and one Supabase project holding everything that changes. Either half can be replaced without touching the other.

2 · The three editing layers & who wins

Same content, three doors. Higher layers override lower ones per task; "Revert to built-in" in the edit drawer removes a browser override.

3 · Publishing a change

For anything edited in files (layers 2–3). Browser edits (layer 1) skip this entirely — they're live on save.

4 · How an email alert happens

Everything configurable lives in the dashboard's Alerts tab; everything automatic runs in the cloud. The email log is the audit trail when anything looks off.

5 · When something's wrong, start here

Three symptoms cover nearly every incident. Follow a column top to bottom.
